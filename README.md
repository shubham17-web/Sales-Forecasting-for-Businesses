# 📊 Sales Forecasting for Businesses

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes historical business sales data, identifies sales and profitability patterns, performs SQL analysis, conducts time-series analysis, builds forecasting models, evaluates model performance, and presents the results through interactive Streamlit and Power BI dashboards.

## 🔎 Key Findings

The analysis produced several business insights from the historical sales data:

- **Technology** was the highest-performing category by sales, generating approximately **$836,154** in sales and **$145,455** in profit.
- **West** was the strongest region by sales, generating approximately **$725,458** in sales.
- **Tables** was a major loss-making sub-category, generating approximately **-$17,725** in profit.
- **Furniture** had substantially lower profitability than Technology and Office Supplies, with a profit margin of approximately **2.49%**.
- Higher discount levels were **associated with lower profitability** in the analyzed data.
- **November** was identified as the highest-sales month in the analysis, highlighting an important seasonal sales period.

> The discount analysis shows an association between discount levels and profitability. It does not establish that discounts directly caused lower profits.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build a practical end-to-end business analytics solution using historical sales data.

The project answers questions such as:

- Which categories generate the most sales and profit?
- Which products and sub-categories are loss-making?
- Which regions perform best?
- Which customer segments contribute the most sales?
- How are discounts associated with profitability?
- What are the monthly and yearly sales trends?
- Are there identifiable seasonal patterns?
- Can historical sales be used to forecast future sales?
- Which forecasting approach produces the lowest prediction error?
- What business recommendations can be derived from the analysis?
